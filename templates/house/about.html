About

Maryland. Youngest of four. Played soccer growing up, then in college. Now: films, interfaces, and sessions, made by one pair of hands.

The short version

This is a game. Use it to build a life.

Soccer offered order when the rest of life did not. The field was the place things made sense, and that is still how the work gets made — a set of constraints, worked at until it comes right.

Central midfielder. DeMatha Catholic, then Baltimore Celtic, then Maryland, then Temple. He has been the best player on teams and he has been the one watching. Both are in how he coaches and both are in how he builds.

Favourite player growing up was Ronaldinho — which is the whole point. Joy is not the opposite of standards.

Three trades, one house: Create under Vision Oasis, Build on the web, and Train as FRENDACH.
